Home Assistant

Radar Base appears in Home Assistant by itself through MQTT discovery. You use your own MQTT broker, usually the Mosquitto broker add-on in Home Assistant. Kymopsis runs no broker and no cloud; nothing leaves your network.

What you need

  • Home Assistant with the Mosquitto broker add-on and the MQTT integration, or any other MQTT broker you run.

Note the broker's address, port (usually 1883), user and password.

  • A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, or a dual-band network where 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz share one name. The ESP32-S3 has no

5 GHz radio.

  • Radar Base firmware 0.9.1 or later (install it from the browser).

Set it up

  1. Join your Wi-Fi: open the Radar Base app (over USB, or the board's hotspot), Device settings, Wi-Fi.
  2. In Device settings, Smart home: enter the broker address, port, user and password, then switch MQTT on.
  3. In Home Assistant open Settings, Devices & services, MQTT. Kymopsis Radar Base is there with its entities. Nothing else to configure.

Online and offline

The board publishes an availability topic. When you switch MQTT off on the board, the device turns unavailable at once. If the board loses power or Wi-Fi, the broker marks it unavailable after about 45 seconds; it comes back by itself when the board reconnects.
