Radar Base

Radar Base connects a 60 GHz mmWave radar module to your computer, your phone and your smart home. The radar does the radar work on its own chip; Radar Base loads radar apps into it, turns their results into one simple JSON format, and serves them over USB and Wi-Fi.

Run a ready-made radar appPeople positions in 3D, fall detection, breathing and heart rate. No coding. Use it in the browser over USBPlug in, open this site in Chrome or Edge, done. No Wi-Fi needed. Put it on your home Wi-FiReach it at http://kymradar.local from any phone or computer at home. Build your own integrationRead the results as JSON over USB or WebSocket from any program.
