Use it over USB in the browser

The Radar Base app runs in Chrome or Edge on your computer and talks to the board through the USB cable (Web Serial). Nothing to install, no Wi-Fi involved.

Steps

  1. Connect the controller's USB port to the computer.
  2. Open radar.kymopsis.com/app in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Press Connect USB in the top bar and choose "USB JTAG/serial debug unit".
  4. The status turns green. Pick a task on the home page.

Your browser asks once per site for permission to use the port; nothing else on your computer is shared.

What works over USB

FeatureOver USB
Run a scenario, live views, scenario settingsYes
Raw data settings and the data-link self-testYes
Write firmware to the radar (dev kit)Yes
Set up home Wi-FiYes (network names and passwords without spaces)
Upload or delete firmware filesNo - use Wi-Fi for this

Only one program at a time

A USB port can be open in one program only. Close any serial terminal before connecting the browser, and the other way round.

Browsers

Web Serial is available in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers on desktop. Safari and Firefox do not support it; use Home Wi-Fi on those, or on phones.
