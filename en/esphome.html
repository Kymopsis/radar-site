ESPHome

Planned after the Home Assistant MQTT integration.

An ESPHome external component will let you describe Radar Base in YAML like any other ESPHome device. It will use the same results as the app and MQTT (people, areas, fall, breathing and heart rate), so you can mix them with your own ESPHome sensors and automations.

Until then, use Home Assistant over MQTT, or read the JSON results directly (JSON and API).
